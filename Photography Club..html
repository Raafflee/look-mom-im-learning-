Photography School Club

Capturing Moments, Telling Stories.

About the Club:

This club is a place to learn the basics of photography together with friends. We learn how to choose camera angles, adjust lighting, and create photos that tell a story.

I chose the photography club because this activity trains creativity and attention to detail.

Members can also explore the school grounds while looking for interesting photo subjects.

Our Activities:

1. Learning basic photo-taking techniques
2.Photo hunting around the school grounds
3.Presenting and discussing photo results

What to Bring:

  • Camera or Phone
  • Notebook
  • Wtaer Bottle
  • Enthusiasm to Learn!
